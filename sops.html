Standard operating procedures

I write SOPs so a task gets done the same way every time, by anyone on the team. Here’s the template I use and three sample SOPs built from it.

Process documentationNotionmonday.comLoomGoogle Workspace
Why SOPs matter

Without them, knowledge lives in one person’s head. Tasks get done differently each time, and training someone new takes weeks.

How I write them

One action per step, starting with a verb. I add the tools needed up front, a quality checklist at the end and fixes for common problems.

What teams get
The same result every time
Faster training for new team members
Work that keeps running when someone is away

The template

Every SOP I write follows the same structure, so anyone on the team can pick one up and follow it.

Three sample SOPs

Written from the template for tasks I do every week. Recreated as samples, since my client SOPs are private.

SEO

SOP: SEO site audit in Ahrefs

Sample
SOP ID
SEO-001
Owner
Marketing administrator
How often
New websites, then monthly
Time needed
45–60 minutes
Purpose

Find and prioritize technical and on-page SEO issues on a website, so fixes can be scheduled.

Tools & access

Ahrefs Site Audit project for the domain, the audit spreadsheet and the team’s task board.

Steps
1 Confirm the website is set up as a project in Ahrefs, and check the crawl settings cover the right domain.
2 Run a new crawl in Site Audit and wait for it to finish.
3 Record the Health Score and the number of errors, warnings and notices in the audit spreadsheet.
4 Review errors first: broken pages, server errors, broken internal links and redirect chains.
5 Review on-page issues: missing or duplicate titles and meta descriptions, missing H1s and images without alt text.
6 Log each issue with its URL, the problem, a priority (high, medium or low) and the suggested fix.
7 Share the prioritized fix list with the team and create a task for every high-priority issue.
8 Run a new crawl after fixes and record the new Health Score with the date.
Quality check
Every high-priority issue has a task
Each issue in the list has a URL
Health Score recorded with the date
Troubleshooting

Crawl stops early? Check that the site isn’t blocking the crawler, then run it again.

Too many issues? Fix the ones affecting the most pages first. Notices are rarely urgent.

Web content

SOP: Publishing a blog post in WordPress

Sample
SOP ID
WEB-002
Owner
Marketing administrator
How often
Each approved post
Time needed
30–45 minutes
Purpose

Publish blog posts that look consistent, read well on any device and are easy for search engines to understand.

Tools & access

WordPress editor access, the approved draft, the target keyword, the featured image and the content tracker.

Steps
1 Get the approved draft, target keyword and featured image.
2 Create a new post and paste the text into the editor without extra formatting.
3 Use the title as the only H1, with H2 and H3 headings in order.
4 Use the target keyword naturally in the title, the first paragraph and one heading.
5 Compress images, give them descriptive file names and add alt text.
6 Add two or three internal links to related posts or service pages.
7 Set the URL slug, category, tags and featured image.
8 Write the SEO title (about 60 characters) and meta description (about 155 characters).
9 Preview on desktop and mobile, then publish or schedule.
10 Add the live link to the content tracker and share it with the team.
Quality check
No broken links
Images load and have alt text
Mobile preview checked
Content tracker updated
Troubleshooting

Formatting looks wrong after pasting? Paste as plain text and add headings again.

Image too heavy? Resize it to the width of the content area before uploading.

Executive support

SOP: Daily inbox triage (zero inbox)

Sample
SOP ID
EA-003
Owner
Executive assistant
How often
Twice a day, 9 AM and 3 PM
Time needed
20–30 minutes
Purpose

Keep the executive’s inbox at zero, so nothing urgent is missed and every request has a next step.

Tools & access

Delegated email and calendar access, the VIP sender list, saved reply templates and the task board.

Steps
1 Scan new emails for senders on the VIP list and handle those first.
2 Sort every email into one of four actions: do it now, delegate it, schedule it or archive it.
3 Reply to routine requests using saved templates.
4 Forward emails to the right team member with a short note and a deadline.
5 Turn emails that need action into tasks with due dates, and label emails that are waiting on a reply.
6 Add meetings and deadlines to the calendar and confirm the invites.
7 File receipts and documents into their labels, and unsubscribe from newsletters nobody reads.
8 Send the executive a short summary of anything that needs their decision.
Quality check
Inbox at zero
Every action email has a task
Summary sent to the executive
Troubleshooting

Not sure if something is urgent? Flag it in the summary rather than guess.

Same question keeps coming in? Write a new saved reply for it.
