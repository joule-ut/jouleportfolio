Case study · Email marketing

Email automation

Nine Mailchimp customer journeys and fifteen branded emails for three sample brands I created: a café, a real estate agency and a luxury fashion house. Each one turns a new subscriber or lead into a visit, a booking or a sale.

MailchimpCustomer JourneysTags & segmentationEmail designCopywriting
The challenge

Turn new subscribers and leads into bookings and visits automatically, with emails that feel personal and match each brand.

My role

I planned every journey, set up the triggers, tags and waits, and wrote and designed every email. When Mailchimp’s nurture template needed an online store, I built that series from scratch.

Key results
9 automated customer journeys across 3 brands
15 branded emails, written and designed
Journeys triggered by sign-ups, landing pages and tags
Unsubscribes and bounces archived automatically

Workflows

Journey 1

Welcome new subscribers

Trigger Someone signs up

Journey 2

Engaged subscriber follow-up

Trigger Opens the welcome email
Tag Engaged
Wait 3 days
Email Four garden roasts, picked for you
Tag High Engagement
Wait 3 days
Email Walk the coffee terraces with us this Saturday

Linked to journey 1, so engaged readers move straight into this flow.

Journey 3

Archive unsubscribed & cleaned

Trigger Unsubscribes or email bounces
Action Archive the contact

Keeps the list clean and the sending reputation healthy.
