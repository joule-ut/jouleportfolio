Issues found

PriorityIssuePagesExample URLFix
High Broken internal links (404) 4 /journal/how-to-price-your-home-to-sell/ Update the links to point to live listing pages.
High Redirect chains 3 /about-us/ Link straight to the final URL instead of through two redirects.
High Pages missing a title tag 2 /buyer-consultation/ Write a unique title of about 60 characters for each page.
Medium Duplicate meta descriptions 6 /listings/ Write a unique description for each page.
Medium Missing H1 heading 2 /contact/ Add one clear H1 that describes the page.
Medium Images without alt text 12 /journal/ Add short, descriptive alt text to each image.
Medium Large images slowing pages 5 /listings/ Compress images to under 200 KB before uploading.
Low Meta descriptions too long 9 /journal/staging-tips-…/ Trim to about 155 characters.
Low Orphan pages (no internal links) 3 /home-valuation/ Link to them from the menu or related posts.
