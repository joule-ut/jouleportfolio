A B2B lead generation plan for a Singapore software company

A full plan for Tidewell HR, a fictional HR and payroll software company that sells to small and medium businesses in Singapore. It covers the lead magnet, landing page, nurture emails, lead scoring and CRM stages.

The company

Tidewell HR sells cloud HR and payroll software to Singapore businesses with 10–200 employees.

Who decides

The HR manager uses it, the finance lead checks the cost, and the founder gives the final yes.

The goal

Turn website and LinkedIn visitors into qualified leads, and book more product demos for the sales team.

Where the leads come from

LinkedIn
Posts from the founder’s profile, 3 times a week
Short tips from the guide, with a link to download
LinkedIn ads aimed at HR and finance roles in Singapore
Google search
Blog posts on specific questions, like Employment Pass salary rules
Keywords chosen in Google Keyword Planner, favoring specific phrases with clear intent
New posts submitted for indexing in Google Search Console
Webinars & partners

Why score leads?

Not every download is a buyer. Scoring shows sales who is ready to talk, so they spend time on the best leads.

50 points = Marketing Qualified Lead

At 50 points, HubSpot moves the lead to MQL and assigns it to a salesperson.

CRM lifecycle stages

How a lead moves through HubSpot, from first download to customer.

STAGE 1 Subscriber Downloaded the guide or joined the newsletter
STAGE 2 Lead Gave work details: company, size and role
STAGE 3 MQL Lead score reaches 50 or more
STAGE 4 SQL Sales confirms they have a need, budget and timing
STAGE 5 Opportunity Demo booked and a proposal is in progress
STAGE 6 Customer Signed up and onboarded
