Website pages & blog posts

Thirteen website pages and twelve blog posts across three sample WordPress sites I built, each written and styled to match its brand. Every link opens the live page.

WordPressPage buildingBlog writingOn-page SEOBrand styling
The challenge

Give each business the pages visitors look for, plus a blog that answers real customer questions and helps the site get found.

My role

I built the pages, set up a Journal for each site, and published posts with clear headings, photos, captions and links to related articles.

Key results
13 website pages across 3 brands
12 blog posts in 3 different brand styles
Journals linked from each site’s menu and footer

The sites run live in your browser through WordPress Playground, so pages can take a few seconds to load. Best opened in Chrome or Safari.
