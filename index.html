Case study 2 · Animal wellness

A planned social media calendar and a fully documented business

Real client work
The challenge

A veterinary herbalist needed to show up on social media consistently, and to have every part of her business written down so nothing depended on memory.

What I did
Planned her social media week by week: how many posts, on which days and on which platforms

Drafted the posts, ready for her review
Wrote an SOP or guide for every part of the business, down to the smallest task

Reviewed her website and social media from a customer’s point of view

A social media plan with no guessing about what to post next
