Social media content calendar

A one-month plan for Solana Coffee & Kitchen, my sample café brand, for December 2026. Every post has a set day, platform, format, content pillar, topic, caption and call to action.

Content strategyContent pillarsCopywritingInstagramFacebookScheduling
Goal

Grow local awareness over the holidays, sell gift sets and fill the Saturday Garden Tours.

Audience

Local coffee lovers, families and remote workers who want a café with a story.

Posting rhythm

3 posts a week on Instagram and Facebook: Monday, Wednesday and Friday. 13 posts this month.

What I’d track

Reach, saves, shares, link clicks and Garden Tour bookings.

Keyword research

Keywords come from Google Keyword Planner. I skip huge terms with 100,000+ monthly searches and favor specific phrases with 10–100, where the right people are searching and there’s less competition.

Content pillars

From the garden31%
Mondays · Carousels

Teaches followers how Solana grows and roasts its coffee. Educational posts get saved and shared.

Behind the bar38%
